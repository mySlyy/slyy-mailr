Slyy Mailr 0.7.1 — snooze & remind

- Snooze & remind: when a mail mentions a date ("I'll be back on Monday December 7th", "bis 4. Dezember"), the reader offers a reminder for that day. Pick the date and time; the mail leaves the list and comes back unread with a notification. "Snooze…" is also in the "…" menu and the right-click menu. Snoozes stay on the device where you set them.
- Drafts keep their meeting invitation and video link when you reopen them from the Drafts folder.
- After sleep or a network change, Mailr reconnects quietly instead of showing "Socket is not connected".
