Slyy Mailr 0.8.3 — junk, images, keys

- Not Junk: in the Junk folder, "Not Junk" (reader, right-click, "…") moves the mail to the Inbox and trusts the sender; their mail that lands in Junk again moves to the Inbox by itself.
- Remote images: "Always from this sender" next to "Load images".
- Press U to mark the current message read or unread.
- Replies: your text is only as tall as what you wrote; the quoted mail gets the rest of the window.
- Settings: the gear opens the Settings window, which can be resized. The meeting link shows which link new meetings use, Test call uses the link in the field (saving it first), and the separate "Room Links" page is gone.
