Slyy Mailr 0.3.1 — settings in sections, default sender, your reply colour, auto-save

**Settings**: Mail, Calendar and Video Calls each list their sections on the left (Mail: Account, Sender
Addresses, Reading and Writing, Assistant, Servers, Updates). Every section saves on its own; only server
changes and a new password reconnect. Esc closes Settings.

**Default sender**: in Sender Addresses, mark one address (the account address or an alias) as Default. New mails
start from it, with its signature. Replies still go out from the address a mail was sent to; mail that names none
of your addresses (mailing lists, Bcc) is answered from the default.

**Your reply colour**: pick the colour your replies are written in (Reading and Writing). Default: the Slyy dot blue.

**Auto-save** (on by default): mails you are writing, replies and drafts are kept on this device a second after
every change and in your Drafts folder every 10 seconds. If Mailr or the Mac crashes, they reopen at the next start.

Installed 0.2.1 or newer on the Mac? This update arrives by itself. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
