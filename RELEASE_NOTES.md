Slyy Mailr 0.7.0 — a better writer, reading by message, meeting invitations

Writing
- The writer opens in its own window on the Mac: resize it and keep reading while you write.
- Add a meeting invitation with the calendar icon: title, date, time, duration, video on or off. On Send, everyone in To and Cc is invited and the meeting goes into your calendar.
- The video button is an icon now, and the link in the mail can be edited.
- Your own meeting link: set it once in Settings → Video Calls (e.g. https://vid.rocket.re-cloud.io/YourName) and new mails, meetings and "Meet now" use it.
- Esc closes a message you haven't changed, without asking. The "Save as draft?" prompt is in Slyy style; Return saves.
- A quieter footer: only when the draft was saved.

Reading
- "…" on every message: reply, reply all or forward just that one.
- Select text first, and the reply or forward quotes only that.
- Double-click a message header to open just that message in its own window.

Fixes
- Cancelling one date of a repeating meeting no longer deletes the whole series.
- Cancellations and replies are only applied when they come from the organizer or that attendee.
- Archiving or deleting no longer erases other mails marked for deletion on some servers.
- Autosave no longer leaves extra draft copies, and a dropped connection no longer saves a sent mail twice.
- Fixed a crash on malformed server replies, mixed-up inline images, and connections left open by the new-mail watcher.
