Slyy Mailr 0.8.2 — your colour everywhere

- Your text is written in the colour you picked in Settings in every mail: new mails, forwards and reopened drafts too, not only replies. The quoted original keeps its normal colour. (Settings → Mail → Reading and Writing: "Write my text in colour".)
- ⌥⌘U checks for updates and shows the result right away (also in the Slyy Mailr menu).
