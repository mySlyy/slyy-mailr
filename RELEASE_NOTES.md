Slyy Mailr 0.3.2 — tidier Settings

The section list in Settings (Account, Sender Addresses, …) sits compactly at the top of the left pane instead of
spreading over the whole window.

New in 0.3.1: Settings in sections that save on their own, a default sender address, your own reply colour
(default the Slyy dot blue), and auto-save that brings back unsent mails after a crash.

Installed 0.2.1 or newer on the Mac? This update arrives by itself. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
