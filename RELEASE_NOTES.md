Slyy Mailr 0.6.0 — Haven, the AI agent, in writer, reader and search

With Slyy Haven installed, Mailr's assistant does real work for you. It always shows what it did, cites the mails it used,
and never sends, saves or changes anything by itself.

**In the writer**: one click on *Write the reply*, *Shorten*, *More formal*, *Translate to English* or *Add facts from my
data*. The proposal appears in your reply colour: *Replace my text*, *Insert below* or *Discard*.

**In the reader**: *Summarize thread*, *Draft a reply*, *Research the sender*, *Find related mails*, *Create a task* or
*Schedule a meeting*. You get a short answer with numbered sources [1] [2] (click to open the mail) and proposals: a reply,
a meeting to review in the calendar, or a task for a connected system such as Odoo, which is written only after you confirm.

**Ask across all mail**: type a question in the search field and press ⌘↩ (or click *Ask Haven this question across your
mail*). Haven searches Inbox, Sent and Archive, reads what matters and answers with sources. Follow-up questions work too.

**What Haven did** is listed under every answer (e.g. "mail · searched “Schott Montage”, 9 hits", "odoo · read quotation").
Haven reads your mail through Mailr on this device; data sources and models are set up in Slyy Haven.
